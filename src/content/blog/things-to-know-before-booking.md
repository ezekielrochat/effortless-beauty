## What do I need to know? A letter to brides before booking

Hello gorgeous! Congratulations! That time has come; you are booking your glam team for your dream day.

As a bride, the options can be overwhelming. As an industry professional, it is my goal to make things as simple as possible for you.

## 1. Understanding your choices

Consider key decisions: Airbrush or conventional? Preview or not? Extensions? Wedding day schedule? Solo stylist or team? Simplify your choices by consulting an experienced professional or established company for assistance.

A recent bride’s story illustrates the value of working with a professional team. She booked a vendor with a $7 deposit based on distance. As her wedding neared, communication vanished, and she discovered a calendar glitch leading to a double booking. She sought reliability and found Effortless Beauty.

The lesson: quality matters. You’re investing in more than day-of glam. It’s peace of mind, clear communication, tailored advice, and skilled, hygienic execution.

## 2. Know your style (and what style your vendor provides)

Welcome to the South — or as I like to call it, Dolly Parton Land. Big hair, bright lipstick, and caked-on foundation can still be the norm around these parts.

Not to say I don’t love a full-glam look, but it’s important to find a timeless look that feels like you. That’s why one of the first steps in our booking process is to ask for your wedding Pinterest board.

If you’re not quite sure what you want, we recommend a bridal preview. We also send a questionnaire before services so we can learn what matters most to you.

## 3. Understand the professional standards

**Dress the part.** No matter how early the call time, stylists should maintain a professional appearance. How we present ourselves sets the tone for the entire experience.

**Sanitation is not an option.** Cost does not always reflect quality. At Effortless Beauty, everything we use and do for our clients mirrors the standards we would expect for ourselves.

## 4. A bride’s timeline is final

A bride, her photographer, and her planner put hours into the perfect timeline. It is imperative that stylists keep this timeline in mind. At Effortless Beauty, we create customized timelines hand in hand with your planner.

## 5. A good consultation is everything

Almost every bride wants makeup that looks “natural.” The problem can be what natural means — Kim Kardashian to one person, mascara and tinted moisturizer to another. Finding your true preferences is all in the consultation.

If you opt for a trial, bring reference photos, communicate likes and dislikes, and wear a white shirt similar to your wedding dress.
