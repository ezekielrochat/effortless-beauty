## How to decide

As you decide how to wear your hair on your wedding day, you might be overwhelmed with possibilities. Pinterest, your mom, your fiancé — it seems everyone has an opinion. Ultimately, your opinion matters the most.

In years to come, you want to look back on your photos and be glad you chose the timeless style that flatters your hair, face shape, and dress.

## Know your hair

Knowing your hair and its capabilities is the most important part of choosing a hairstyle. If your hair can’t hold a curl and you want a curly down style during a hot summer, you may want to consider an updo. Soft, elegant updos have come a long way.

If your hair holds curl well and your locks cannot be contained, consider a more casual or boho look, glam waves, or all down with soft curls.

## Face shape

Leaving some pieces down around your face — affectionately named “wispies” — is recommended for most hairstyles. These pieces flatter almost any face shape and add softness. They can also hide your ears if you’d rather.

If you have a more angular face shape and want a more formal style, a sleek updo can be the perfect option.

## Dress to match

If a lot is going on with your dress, keep the hair simpler. Higher necklines or intricate detailing on the back of your dress can be reasons to put it up. If you want movement, leave it down.

Choose your veil and accessories so they complement the dress. We do not want multiple things fighting for attention.

## What it really comes down to

Your opinion matters most. Helping you feel content, happy, and beautiful in your own skin is what we strive to do at Effortless Beauty. If that means wearing leopard print from head to toe, we are here to support you.
